1. Project Overview

About SikhStreet

SikhStreet is envisioned as the world's leading digital commerce ecosystem dedicated to the global Sikh community.

The platform will connect buyers and sellers across countries while enabling highly localized discovery through an advanced geo-location architecture.

Unlike traditional marketplaces, SikhStreet will combine:

  • Global eCommerce Marketplace
  • Hyperlocal Commerce (Example: India -> Karnataka -> Bangalore -> HSR)
  • Community Commerce
  • Verified Vendor Ecosystem
  • Cross-Border Commerce

The platform is intended to become the digital economic infrastructure of the Sikh community globally and must therefore be architected for long-term scalability and future expansion.

Reference: Etsy

5. Advanced Geo-Commerce Engine

This is the primary differentiator of SikhStreet and must be considered a core platform capability.

Hierarchical Location Structure at Global Level

The platform must support:

Country
→ State / Province
→ Region
→ City
→ Area
→ Sector / Locality
→ Street

Example:

India
→ Punjab
→ Mohali
→ Sector 70

The hierarchy must be configurable by administrators and extensible to support all countries globally.

Geo-Based Vendor Discovery

The platform shall prioritize vendors based on proximity and configured marketplace rules.

Example:

Buyer Location:
Mohali Sector 70

Results Priority:

  1. Sector 70 Vendors
  2. Nearby Sectors
  3. Mohali Vendors
  4. Punjab Vendors
  5. India Vendors
  6. International Vendors

The ranking logic must be configurable by administrators.

Street View Marketplace Mode

Unique marketplace browsing mode enabling:

  • Street-level navigation
  • Hyperlocal discovery
  • Distance-based sorting
  • Nearby vendor exploration
  • Location-based recommendations

Traditional Marketplace Mode

Traditional browsing experience including:

  • Category Navigation
  • Search
  • Product Recommendations
  • Vendor Recommendations
  • Featured Products
